# Example Health Plan — Pharmacy Prior Authorization Policy

**Policy:** EHP-RX-0421 · GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Type 2 Diabetes
**Drug covered by this policy:** semaglutide injection (Ozempic®)
**Effective date:** January 1, 2026 · **Version:** 1.1 (see revision history)

> Example Health Plan is fictional. This policy was written for a software demo, modeled on the structure of public payer drug policies. It is not a real coverage policy and not medical guidance.

## 1. Purpose

Semaglutide is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist that lowers blood glucose in adults with type 2 diabetes. Because lower-cost first-line therapy is effective for many members, Example Health Plan requires prior authorization so that semaglutide is used when first-line therapy has been tried and blood glucose remains above goal.

## 2. Initial authorization criteria

Semaglutide is considered medically necessary when **all** of the following criteria are met. The prescriber must submit documentation supporting each criterion.

**C1. Age.** The member is 18 years of age or older.

**C2. Diagnosis.** The member has a diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10-CM E11.x). Semaglutide is not covered under this policy for chronic weight management in members without type 2 diabetes; requests for weight management are reviewed under a separate policy.

**C3. Glycemic control.** The member's most recent hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) is 7.0% or higher, and the result is dated within the last 180 days. If no HbA1c result from the last 180 days is submitted, the request will be pended until a current result is provided.

**C4. Step therapy.** The member has tried metformin for at least 90 days, OR has a documented intolerance or contraindication to metformin (for example, severe gastrointestinal side effects, or reduced kidney function with eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73 m²). An intolerance or contraindication must be described in the clinical notes. If the record shows neither a metformin trial of at least 90 days nor a documented intolerance or contraindication, this criterion is not met.

**C5. No exclusions.** The member does not have a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC) and does not have multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2). Semaglutide carries a boxed warning for thyroid C-cell tumors. The prescriber attests to this criterion by signing the request form, so the review checks the record only for contrary evidence: the criterion is not met if the record documents MTC (personal or family history) or MEN 2, and is met otherwise.

## 3. Required documentation

- Diagnosis code for type 2 diabetes.
- The most recent HbA1c result with its date.
- Medication history showing metformin use and its duration, or clinical notes describing intolerance or contraindication.
- Prescriber attestation, on the request form, that the member has no personal or family history of MTC and no MEN 2 (see C5).

## 4. Approval duration

Initial approval: 6 months. Reauthorization requires a follow-up HbA1c showing a reduction from baseline, or documentation that the member has reached their glycemic goal.

## 5. Not covered

- Use for weight loss alone in members without type 2 diabetes (see C2).
- Use in type 1 diabetes.
- Use together with another GLP-1 receptor agonist.
